FAQ

Buying

How do I buy?

Click a Buy button. Checkout runs on Gumroad — you pay there, and Gumroad sends you the download. We never see your card details.

Do I need an account on this site?

No. There are no accounts here. Gumroad may ask for an email so it can send your download link.

Are there subscriptions or extra charges?

No. Every product is a one-time purchase. There are no per-user fees and no upsells after checkout.

Download and use

What do I get after I buy?

An instant download (ZIP) with the product files, plus a download link by email from Gumroad. The trackers and sheets work in Excel and Google Sheets; the guides are Word/Markdown files.

My download link doesn’t work. What now?

Email uberlin666@gmail.com with your order email and the product name, and we’ll re-send the files.

Do I need Excel?

No. Upload the tracker to Google Drive and open it with Sheets. The scripts are plain Word/Markdown files.

Can I use the tools for jobs other than remodeling?

The scripts mention remodeling jobs, but the tracker, the cadence, and the objection approach work for any trade that sends written quotes. You’ll need to adapt the job-specific wording.

Results

Does the Lead Kit guarantee sales?

No. It guarantees a system and a refund, not income. It makes sure no quote is forgotten, every follow-up has something worth saying, and every price objection has an honest answer. Some quotes will still go nowhere.

Are the example prices in the products real prices I should charge?

No. Worked examples use US market rates as illustrations. Re-price every job with your own supplier prices and labor rates.

How do I get a refund?

How do I ask about an order?

Email uberlin666@gmail.com with your order email and the product name. See Contact.
